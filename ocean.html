About This Series

A lone diver descends into the deepest ocean monument ever constructed in Minecraft — only to discover it is not abandoned. Deep Ocean Saga is a feature-length machinima film blending survival horror with a heartfelt story about loneliness and discovery.

Filmed across a custom-built 1:1 scale ocean biome over 6 months, every frame is meticulously composed like a real underwater documentary.
