About This Series

Told entirely from the perspective of an Enderman who has gained the ability to perceive time and emotion, Enderman Diaries is a meditative, deeply philosophical machinima unlike anything else on the platform.

Each episode follows "Em" as they experience a different dimension, trying to understand the strange creatures called humans — and why they keep making eye contact.
