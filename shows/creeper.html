About This Series

In a world where creepers have gained sentience, one outcast named Sizzle fights to prove his kind deserves a place among the Overworld's greatest heroes. Betrayed by his own kin and hunted by Steve's militia, Sizzle must forge unlikely alliances across every biome to survive.

An epic three-season saga filmed entirely in vanilla Minecraft with zero mods, featuring over 200 individual actors and 14 months of production. Season 3 brings the story to its earth-shattering conclusion.
